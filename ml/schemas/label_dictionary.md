# Nuvii AI Label Dictionary

Use the exact snake_case labels below to keep captions and metadata consistent.

## Shape

- `almond` — Smooth tapered sidewalls ending in a rounded point.
- `stiletto` — Long dramatic taper ending in a sharp point.
- `square` — Straight sidewalls with a flat tip.
- `soft_square` — Square silhouette with softly rounded corners.
- `coffin` — Tapered sidewalls with a flat narrow tip.
- `oval` — Rounded tip with gently curved sidewalls.
- `round` — Short rounded free edge with minimal taper.
- `squoval` — Hybrid square and oval silhouette.
- `ballerina` — Coffin-like taper with a broader flat tip.

## Length

- `xs` — Extra-short press-on length.
- `short` — Short practical length.
- `medium` — Moderate everyday length.
- `long` — Long statement length.
- `xl` — Extra-long editorial length.

## Finish

- `glossy` — High-shine clear topcoat appearance.
- `matte` — Velvet flat finish with minimal reflection.
- `jelly` — Translucent colored gel appearance.
- `glazed` — Subtle pearlescent glazed-donut sheen.
- `pearl` — Soft luminous pearl reflection.
- `chrome` — Reflective metallic pigment or isolated chrome detail.
- `glitter` — Visible glitter particles.
- `shimmer` — Fine light-reflective particles.
- `magnetic` — Cat-eye or velvet magnetic gel effect.
- `translucent` — Partially transparent base or color.

## 2D Technique

- `french_tip` — Tip color separated by a curved smile line.
- `curved_french` — Rounded French tip following a deep curved smile line.
- `reverse_french` — Accent curve placed near the cuticle.
- `ombre` — Gradual blend between colors or opacity levels.
- `aura` — Centered airbrushed gradient that radiates outward.
- `marble` — Fluid stone-like veining and blended color movement.
- `isolated_chrome` — Chrome applied only to drawn lines or specific motifs.
- `chrome_outline` — Metallic outline surrounding a shape, French edge, or motif.
- `foil` — Irregular metallic foil fragments or veins.
- `cat_eye` — Magnetic reflective line, velvet, or galaxy effect.
- `negative_space` — Intentional exposed base areas within the composition.
- `floral_paint` — Painted floral or botanical motif.
- `animal_print` — Cheetah, leopard, zebra, tortoise, or similar pattern.
- `glitter_border` — Fine glitter framing a French edge or nail perimeter.

## 3D Technique

- `3d_swirl` — Raised gel line forming curves or loops.
- `sculpted_shell` — Raised ridged shell or ripple relief.
- `raised_flower` — Dimensional flower built above the nail surface.
- `3d_petals` — Raised individual petals or wing-like forms.
- `raised_bow` — Dimensional gel or charm bow.
- `bubble_accents` — Rounded clear or colored gel bubbles.
- `gel_droplets` — Raised teardrop or raindrop-shaped gel.
- `raised_butterfly` — Dimensional butterfly or wing structure.
- `raised_outline` — Thick gel line outlining a motif.

- `butterfly` — Butterfly body or wing motif.

- `swirl` — Curved decorative line or loop.

- `shell` — Shell, ridge, or ocean-inspired motif.

- `celestial` — Stars, moons, galaxies, or cosmic themes.

## Embellishment

- `rhinestone` — Faceted crystal nail stone.
- `pearl` — Pearl bead or pearl cluster.
- `caviar_beads` — Tiny metallic microbeads applied individually or in lines.
- `chain` — Fine metal chain draped across the nail.

- `gem` — Colored or clear gemstone element.

- `metallic_ring` — Raised circular ring or frame element.
- `clear_bubble` — Transparent raised bubble or orb.

- `none` — No physical embellishment.

## Placement

- `full_nail` — Design covers most or all of the nail.
- `tip` — Primary design is concentrated at the free edge.
- `cuticle` — Primary design is concentrated near the cuticle.
- `center` — Primary design is centered.
- `side` — Design is concentrated on one side.
- `border` — Design frames the perimeter or an internal shape.
- `diagonal` — Design crosses the nail diagonally.
- `scattered` — Elements are distributed across the nail.
- `left_side` — Design is weighted to the left side.
- `right_side` — Design is weighted to the right side.

## Density

- `detailed` — High detail and layered techniques.
- `maximal` — Dense, heavily embellished, statement design.
